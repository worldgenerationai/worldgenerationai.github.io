On 29 June 2029, we hosted an online Expert Roundtable, bringing together research leaders to help shape a shared agenda for advancing children's agency in the age of AI. We were honoured to be joined by 11 experts to explore two core questions:
- Agency conceptualisation: What is your definition of agency in the context of children and their interaction with AI? What do you think of CHAILD's working conceptual framework?
- Evaluation and evidence: How should agency be assessed, and what do you think of our current assessment strategy?

The two-hour discussion was highly engaging and generated rich feedback from all participating experts. We are now preparing a summary of the discussions and plan to share the roundtable report by early September.

This successful event would not have been possible without the support and dedication of every CHAILD team member and each participating expert.
