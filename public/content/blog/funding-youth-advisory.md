We are excited to announce funding has been awarded to establish a youth advisory group focused on AI and data science projects at Oxford.

Isobel Voysey will lead one of five [Public and Community Engagement with Research (PCER) Fellowships](https://www.mpls.ox.ac.uk/public-engagement/pcer-fellows-scheme-25-26) awarded for 2025–26 by Oxford's Mathematical, Physical and Life Sciences division, for a project titled *Pilot of an AI and Data Science Youth Advisory Group*. The fellowships run for the academic year and support researchers and staff in embedding public and community engagement across their departments. Isobel and Vid worked on this proposal to create more opportunities to engage with youth in our research.

Youth advisory groups of this kind already exist in Medical Sciences and in Social Sciences departments at the university, but this will be the first in MPLS.

This group will:

- Bring young people's perspectives into AI research
- Co-design research materials
- Ensure research is relevant, responsible, and impactful

We hope that it will be a worthwhile experience for the young people and us too!
