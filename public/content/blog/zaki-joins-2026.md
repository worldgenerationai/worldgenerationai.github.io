**Dr Zaki Pauzi** has joined CHAILD as a Research Fellow at the UCL Knowledge Lab, supervised by Professor Manolis Mavrikis.

![Zaki, Manolis and Vid standing together in front of a large geodesic sculpture at the UCL Knowledge Lab](/content/blog/images/ucl-team-2026.jpg "The UCL side of the CHAILD team: Zaki, Manolis and Vid")

That completes the UCL team in the project. Manolis has led it from the start, Vid [took up a joint post here in August 2025](/blog/vid-joins-ucl-2025/) alongside his Oxford role and is supervised by Manolis on this side too, and Zaki now joins them.

Zaki is no stranger to the building or the CHAILD team - he was previously already working with Manolis on evaluating educational technology in the [Transforming Education with Emerging Technologies (TransEET) project](https://transeet.eu/).

He arrives with a PhD in computer software engineering from the University of Groningen and a degree in computer science and management from King's College London — and, less usually for a postdoc, a day job alongside it: he has been a senior data scientist at bp since 2019.

His recent research, including [a paper](/publications/) on whether a language model can judge the pedagogical quality of a conversational agent as reliably as a human can, is highly relevant to the CHAILD project. Zaki's expertise will be highly valuable for helping the team find ways to assess children's agency and evaluate to what extent AI tools support it.

Welcome, Zaki.
